# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not people. Assume the other person read the same
docs you did and reached a different conclusion for a reason.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement, stated plainly, with evidence.
- Reviews that name the problem and, where you can, the fix.
- Patience with people who are new to the repo. Everything here is unusual.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, issues, or review.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you are
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/umgbhalla/desmos/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block repeat offenders.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
